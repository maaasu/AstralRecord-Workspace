### AR-CODE-001 [中] 姿勢解除の例外でAI停止が残る

- 関連箇所: `10_plugin/AstralRecord/src/main/java/io/github/maaasu/astralRecord/feature/mob/service/MobAiService.java:265`

- 修正対象候補: `10_plugin/AstralRecord/src/main/java/io/github/maaasu/astralRecord/feature/mob/skill/field/ShadeHedgehogMobSkillExecutor.java`
